Summary

About

I work across research, policy analysis, and academic mentoring, with fieldwork experience spanning UNDP, UN Women, and civil-society governance initiatives in Bangladesh. My research centers on housing, urban poverty, and the political economy of social protection — using mixed-method frameworks to trace how formal and informal systems shape access for households.

I graduated top of my batch in both the Master's and Bachelor's programs in Sociology at the University of Dhaka, and I'm currently a Trainee Research Associate on the Young Researchers' Fellowship at BIGD, BRAC University.
